# Trading 212 strategies: audit and backtest findings, 24 Sep 2026

Scope: the live T212 bot (`212trading/t212_bot.py`, one thread per `config.<asset>.env`) running
NVDA, SPCX and RGNT with `212trading/strategy.py`, its live logs and state, the account, and a
backtest grid through the live engine (`212trading/replay.py`). Reproduce the grid with
`fetch.py`, `grid.py` and `report.py` in this directory (a few minutes on DEV).

## 1. The account cannot fund the configured budgets (configuration, critical)

| | value |
|---|---|
| account currency | RON |
| account total | 7987 RON (~1717 USD) |
| free cash | 408 RON (~88 USD) |
| configured budgets (`STRAT_MAX_BUDGET`, USD) | NVDA 3000 + RGNT 1300 + SPCX 4000 = 8300 USD |
| NVDA entry alone (15% of 3000) | 450 USD, ~5x the free cash |

The three strategies compete for the same small cash and most buys are refused:

- July: 1384 "insufficient funds" refusals. The RGNT DCA could not buy at 3.64, 3.47 or 2.54
  and filled only at 2.09 on 20 Jul.
- NVDA sold its position on 15 Jul at ~212.74. Its re-entry condition was met on 24-26 Aug
  (price at or below 209.55), and every ENTRY was refused for insufficient funds. NVDA is now
  at ~225.
- The account also holds two positions no bot manages: AVGO (-150 USD) and CATS, for which T212
  reports a price of 0.00 (-112 USD).

Fix by configuration: set each `STRAT_MAX_BUDGET` to the cash actually allocated to that asset,
so that the three budgets add up to at most the free cash plus the value of the positions.

## 2. The re-entry rule locks positions out of rising markets (strategy)

After a take-profit the engine re-enters only when price falls `STRAT_REENTRY_DROP_PCT` below
the sale price. There is no other way back in. This is the same rule as the Kraken engine and
has the same effect. In continuous backtests the live NVDA profile was in the market 0.1% of the
time and made +0.54% over the last ~730 days of hourly bars (NVDA buy-and-hold: +453%).
Fresh-state 90-day windows hide it: every window starts flat, so live shows 100% positive windows.

## 3. Code defects

| defect | effect | status |
|---|---|---|
| TTL re-placement compared the raw price with the limit (`price > limit * 1.003`) | Every BUY is placed at price minus the discount. With a discount above 0.3% the condition holds at an unchanged price, so the same order was cancelled and re-submitted every TTL (RGNT DCA, 16-17 Jul: 173.28 @ 2.31 every 3 minutes). | fixed, `_buy_is_stale()` |
| Replay never re-placed a stale BUY | a backtest entry left below a rally never filled | fixed, replay uses `_buy_is_stale()` |
| Strategy log lines had no instrument | three assets interleave in one log; events can only be told apart by price level | fixed, lines start with `[nvda]`, `[spcx]`, `[rgnt]` |
| `price_alert.py` loaded only `212trading/.env` | `NTFY_TOPIC_PRICE` moved to the repository `.env`; the 15-minute RGNT alert has exited with "no ntfy topic" 419 times since ~20 Sep | fixed, uses `load_t212_environment()` |
| A partially filled ENTRY is never topped up | once any quantity fills, `step()` takes the holding path and never buys the rest; RGNT's cycle started with 1.4 shares (~11 USD) at 7.80 instead of ~400 USD | open |
| `spent_cash` tracks the cost of the current holding, not the cycle's spending | after a partial ladder sale the per-cycle cap frees up and DCA can exceed `STRAT_MAX_BUDGET` over the cycle | open, minor |

The T212 bot is not affected by the root `config.env` leak that hit Kraken and Hyperliquid: it
reads each profile into a dictionary with no fallback to `os.environ`.

## 4. Backtests

Engine: live `Strategy` through `212trading/replay.py` (with the TTL fix), Yahoo regular-session
bars, 1h over the last ~730 days and 1d over ~10 years, continuous runs. Each profile runs on its
own asset and on 15 other US stocks and ETFs (AAPL, AMD, AMZN, AVGO, COIN, GOOGL, META, MSFT,
NFLX, NVDA, PLTR, QQQ, SPY, TSLA, plus SPCX/RGNT where they are not the home asset). Returns and
drawdowns are % of the profile budget. The DCA trend gate is disabled on 1h/1d (it needs 5m bars).

### NVDA profile on NVDA

| variant | 1h return | 1h DD | 1h time in market | 1d return (10y) | 1d DD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP 12%, re-entry 0, no stop | +115.5 | 17.9 | 99% | +232.2 | 29.8 |
| TP 8%, re-entry 0, no stop | +103.6 | 15.5 | 99% | +220.6 | 28.6 |
| TP 5%, re-entry 0, no stop | +97.5 | 15.7 | 98% | +184.1 | 30.3 |
| re-entry from post-sale peak (else live) | +38.1 | 19.6 | 53% | +9.0 | 53.3 |
| re-entry 0 (else live) | +56.5 | 19.0 | 91% | +18.3 | 50.7 |
| TP 12% alone | +2.9 | 0.5 | 1% | +1.8 | 1.0 |
| **live** (TP 1.5%, re-entry 1.5%, stop 30%) | **+0.5** | 0.1 | 0.1% | **+0.1** | 0.2 |

Changing take-profit, stop or trailing alone does nothing while the re-entry lock stays.

### Every profile on the other 15 assets

| profile | best variant | beats live (1h) | positive (1h) | 1h median | 1d median | live 1h median | live 1d median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NVDA | TP 12%, re-entry 0, no stop | 12/15 | 12/15 | +55.1 | +128.3 | +0.2 | +0.5 |
| SPCX | TP 20%, re-entry 0, no stop | 14/15 | 14/15 | +34.7 | +87.7 | +14.9 | +14.5 |
| RGNT | re-entry 0 (else live) | 12/15 | 15/15 | +28.4 | +83.8 | +10.7 | +10.7 |

- **Re-entry is the main lever** for all three profiles.
- **No stop-loss** raises the medians on the other assets (NVDA profile: +55.1 vs +45.9 on 1h,
  +128.3 vs +92.0 on 1d). On NVDA's own 10-year daily history the 30% stop is slightly better
  (e.g. +254.0 vs +242.6), so this one is a risk choice. The old 12% SPCX stop is where the
  -158 USD realised loss came from.
- **Take-profit of 8-20%** beats 1.5%. NVDA's 1.5% round trips mostly pay FX fees and spread.
- **RGNT** is a special case on its own chart: its live profile (DCA at -40%, TP 35%, no stop)
  made +58% on 1h while the stock fell 72%, and re-entry changes hurt there. SPCX has only three
  months of history, too short to conclude anything.

Caveats: 2023-2026 was a strong period for US equities; the 10-year daily runs include the 2018,
2020 and 2022 drawdowns and keep the same ranking. The backtest funds each profile's full budget,
which the real account cannot do (section 1).

## 5. Recommendations

1. Right-size `STRAT_MAX_BUDGET` per profile to real cash. Nothing else works while every buy is
   refused.
2. Fix the re-entry lock. The simplest tested form is `STRAT_REENTRY_DROP_PCT=0` (re-enter at the
   next entry, which is still placed at the entry discount).
3. For NVDA-style profiles raise the take-profit to 8-12% and consider disabling the stop-loss;
   keep RGNT's profile as it is.
4. Merge `claude/t212-audit` for the TTL churn, the per-asset log labels and the price alert.
5. Decide what to do with the unmanaged AVGO and CATS positions.
