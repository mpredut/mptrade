# Kraken spot-DCA: overnight research findings, 24 Sep 2026

Scope: the Kraken spot-DCA engine (`strategies/spot_dca.py`, live on HYPE, TAO and ADA) replayed
through the live code path (`kraken/replay.py`) on 4h bars. Eleven assets over the widest history
available: Binance USDT spot since 2018 as a price proxy (ADA, AVAX, BNB, BTC, DOGE, ETH, LINK,
SOL, TAO, XRP) plus HYPE Hyperliquid spot (628 days). Fee 0.26% per leg unless stated. Returns and
drawdowns are % of the 3900 USD cycle budget. Reproduce with `run_all.sh` in this directory.

Four views per configuration:

- **full**: one continuous run over the whole history; state carries across cycles, as it does
  live (including the post-take-profit re-entry rule);
- **year**: continuous run per calendar year;
- **w90 / w30**: fresh-state rolling windows of 90 and 30 days, 30-day step, which is what the
  existing walk-forward and pilot tooling measure;
- **cash**: the full run again with a finite 3900 USD account (losses are not refilled).

## 0. Before reading any number: two defects found on the way

1. **The live Kraken and Hyperliquid bots have run in PAPER mode, with a foreign strategy, since
   ~20 Sep 09:57.** Commit 7c719ebe made `log.py` and `accepted_order_persistence.py` load the
   root `config.env` at import time. The root file still held a legacy `STRAT_*` block
   (`STRAT_EXECUTE=false`, take-profit 0.02%, DCA drop 0.01%, entry discount 0.002%), and
   `load_dotenv` never overwrites, so these values replaced `kraken/config.env` and
   `hyperliquid/config.env`. Startup logs say `take-profit: +0.02%` and `[PAPER]`. All pairs were
   flat when it happened and the re-entry rule kept them flat, so no paper fill reached the state
   files. Fix: branch `claude/fix-root-env-leak`. **Merging it restores REAL trading.**
2. **The same leak contaminated the Kraken research runners**: `kraken_walk_forward_baseline.py`,
   `kraken_financial_benchmark.py` and `comprehensive_strategy_sweep.py` replayed that scalper as
   "live". The 23 Sep promotions cite them ("TP 4%: 26/31 positive out-of-sample windows"). Rerun
   with the real live config, `01_tp4` is identical to live (TP is already 4) and nothing
   dominates live on HYPE's fresh-state folds (clean live: mean +1.46%, 25/31 positive).

The grid below loads the configuration through `kraken/shadow_live.py`, which is not affected
(verified: TP 4.0, DCA 1.25, entry 0.8, stop 18).

## 1. The live configuration is almost never in the market

After a take-profit the engine re-enters only once price falls `STRAT_REENTRY_DROP_PCT` (2.2%)
below the sale price. There is no other way back in: if price keeps rising, the bot stays flat
indefinitely. Continuous history shows it; fresh-state windows hide it, because every window
starts flat without a previous sale.

| continuous run | live return | time in market | cycles |
|---|---|---|---|
| HYPE, 628 days (buy & hold +452%) | +5.6% | 1.0% | 8 |
| BTC since 2018 (B&H +425%) | -36.8% | 3.2% | 24 |
| ETH since 2018 (B&H +143%) | -72.5% | 2.3% | 29 |
| 11 assets, median | -9.3% | 2.3% | 19 |

Live is positive on 3 of 11 assets. It is also the state right now: HYPE sold at 56.66 and waits
for 55.41 (price 93), TAO sold at 219.46 (price 288), ADA sold at 0.2215 (price 0.238).

## 3. What works: the trend overlay, and fewer stop-losses

Top configurations by median full-run return/drawdown (11 assets, legacy funding):

| config | beats live | positive assets | full median | full worst | DD median | DD max | w90 mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ov650_t8_sl0 | 11/11 | 11/11 | +116.8 | +53.9 | 40.6 | 78.4 | +3.24 |
| ov650_t8_brake_gateoff | 10/11 | 9/11 | +63.1 | -12.9 | 28.6 | 52.8 | +2.77 |
| ov1000_t8_gateoff | 11/11 | 10/11 | +87.1 | -5.9 | 38.2 | 108.6 | +4.54 |
| ov650_t8_brake | 11/11 | 9/11 | +39.3 | -27.7 | 28.9 | 47.6 | +1.77 |
| ov650_t8_sl25 | 10/11 | 10/11 | +67.2 | -32.8 | 37.5 | 56.1 | +2.19 |
| ov2000_t10 | 10/11 | 10/11 | +155.1 | -65.7 | 51.4 | 118.2 | +6.77 |
| sl0 (live, no stop) | 9/11 | 11/11 | +29.9 | +5.6 | 28.4 | 71.0 | +1.52 |
| live | - | 3/11 | -9.3 | -72.5 | 23.9 | 81.1 | -1.15 |

`ovX_tY` = trend overlay with a top-up of X USD and a Y% trail (`STRAT_TREND_OVERLAY=true`),
`brake` = DCA trend brake, `gateoff` = no TP regime gate, `slN` = stop-loss N% (0 = off).

- The **overlay** is the only family that keeps participating in trends (time in market ~75-80%
  instead of ~2%). It wins on 9-11 of 11 assets across top-ups 650-2000 and trails 8-12%.
  Tight trails (4-6%) and `trend_exit_break=true` are clearly worse.
- **The stop-loss is the main loss driver of this DCA.** Without it the plain engine is positive
  on all 11 assets and the overlay on all 11. The stop crystallises the deepest drawdowns and
  the re-entry after a stop then misses the recovery.
- **Drawdowns are large.** Median 30-40% of budget, and above 100% for the bigger top-ups under
  legacy funding (see section 5 for the finite-cash check).
- **Fees matter for the overlay.** At 0.40% per leg (Kraken's base-tier taker fee, which the
  overlay's market exits pay) ov650_t8 falls from +20.9 to +1.9 median. ov650_t8_gateoff holds
  (+57.5 -> +36.2). Confirm the account's real fee tier.

## 4. Picking parameters from recent backtests does not work

Every 90 days, pick the best of 14 candidates on the previous 365 days (return/max(DD, 5)) and
run it for the next 90 days, strictly out of sample:

| strategy | sum of out-of-sample 90-day returns, all assets |
|---|---|
| ov1000_t10_gateoff (static) | +1360.6 |
| ov2000_t8 (static) | +1212.9 |
| ov650_t8_gateoff (static) | +881.3 |
| **selector (re-optimised every 90 days)** | **+597.0** |
| ov650_t8 (static) | +394.4 |
| pre-promotion (static) | +202.4 |
| live (static) | -242.8 |

The selector beats live but loses to five static overlay configurations: the recent winner does
not persist. This is what the 23 Sep promotions did on one asset and a contaminated baseline.
Choose one robust configuration across assets and regimes; do not re-tune from the last window.

## 6. Recommendations, in order

1. **Decide on the env-leak fix first** (`claude/fix-root-env-leak`). Merging it puts Kraken and
   Hyperliquid back on REAL money with the current (post-23 Sep) configuration. Check the Kraken
   and Hyperliquid balances against `kraken/.state_*.json` and `hyperliquid/.state_HYPE.json`
   before the restart. A safer sequence: merge with `STRAT_EXECUTE=false` in the venue configs,
   confirm the startup logs show TP 4.0 / 5.0, then flip to true.
2. **Do not keep the 23 Sep promotion as is.** At minimum revert TP to 5% and the regime gate.
3. **Address the re-entry lock.** The overlay is the tested remedy (it enters on confirmed
   uptrends regardless of the last sale). Candidates for the shadow, then a small live allocation:
   - `ov650_t8_brake_gateoff`: smallest drawdown among the winners (median 29%, max 53%);
   - `ov650_t8_sl0`: no losing asset in any view, at the cost of deep drawdowns (max ~78%);
   - `ov1000_t10_gateoff`: no losing asset with real Kraken fees and a finite account.
   Both are expressible in `kraken/config.env` (`STRAT_TREND_OVERLAY=true`, `STRAT_TREND_TOPUP=650`,
   `STRAT_TREND_TRAIL_PCT=8`, `STRAT_TP_REGIME_GATE=false`, plus `STRAT_DCA_TREND_BRAKE=true` or
   `STRAT_STOP_LOSS_PCT=0`).
4. **Revisit the stop-loss.** 0% beats 18% on 9 of 11 assets and ties on the other two (HYPE, AVAX:
   the stop never fired there) in the finite-cash run; 25% is the
   middle ground. This is a risk-appetite decision, not a research one.
5. **Measure research on continuous runs as well as windows**, and check the effective params of
   any Kraken runner (TP must print 4.0, not 0.02) until the leak fix is merged.

Also found (TradeAll, Binance): every TradeAll backtest with the Kalman gate made zero trades
since 19 Jul, because the gate aged historical signals against wall time (fixed on this branch;
the quality-signal experiment now trades). The overnight trigger-gate experiments before the fix
are void and are being rerun on DEV (`~/exp/tradeall/`).

Caveats: Binance prices proxy Kraken pairs; one fee model for all fills; HYPE and TAO histories
are short; drawdown % is relative to the cycle budget, not to account equity.
